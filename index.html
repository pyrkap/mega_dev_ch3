Ticket #4471 originally merged 412 reports into one ticket. Analysis of tenant locales and booking forms shows that these reports cover two distinct root causes.

Split into two separate issues. Ticket #4471 is now split into dedicated tickets with accurate report counts.

The Two Problems

  1. Parser reads dates month-first in day-first locales (379 reports)

    The shared date parser expects MM/DD/YYYY. When day-first tenants submit dates through channels without conflicting hints, dates with days ≤ 12 are inverted, or fail if days > 12.

    Tracked in GitHub issue #1: 379 reports.

  2. Booking-v3 form displays MM/DD/YYYY hint in day-first locales (33 reports)

    The booking-v3 form UI shows an MM/DD/YYYY hint under the date field. Users in day-first locales follow the hint, but the form interprets inputs as day-first.

    Tracked in GitHub issue #2: 33 reports.
